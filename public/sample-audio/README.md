# Synthetic audio fixtures

These WAV files were generated with the macOS Samantha voice for repeatable integration checks. They contain authored calculus examples, not recordings of learners or evidence of learning gains. Uploading them through the live path invokes real ElevenLabs transcription and the configured AI assessor.

- `main.wav` explains distance, speed, and the meaning of six meters per second at three seconds.
- `followup-correct.wav` distinguishes seven meters over the next second from instantaneous speed at three seconds.
- `followup-incorrect.wav` deliberately confuses those two quantities.
